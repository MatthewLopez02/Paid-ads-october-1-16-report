🔥 Key Insights & Recommendations

🏆 Apple Search Ads Excellence

ASA delivers the lowest CPA ($20.69) and exceptional CPI ($5.38), demonstrating high-intent users. The strong install-to-account conversion (38.5% vs 17.2% for Meta) indicates superior user quality. Consider increasing ASA budget allocation.

📊 Google Ads Scale

Google dominates volume with 76.5% of new accounts at a competitive CPA of $24.87. While not the cheapest, Google provides consistent, scalable acquisition making it the backbone of paid growth strategy.

🎯 TikTok Efficiency

TikTok achieves the second-best CPA ($21.59) with solid volume (712 accounts). This platform shows promise for efficient growth and warrants increased investment to test scalability.

⚠️ Meta Optimization Needed

Meta has the highest CPA ($31.49) and highest CPI ($56.56) with poor install-to-account conversion. Recommend creative refresh, audience refinement, or budget reallocation to better-performing channels.

🔄 Install-to-Account Funnel

Overall install-to-account conversion is 57.1%, indicating significant drop-off. Focus on onboarding optimization to capture more of the 5,921 users who installed but didn't complete account creation.

💡 Budget Reallocation Strategy

With ASA and TikTok showing strong efficiency, consider shifting 10-15% of Meta budget to these platforms. Maintain Google as primary volume driver while scaling proven winners.

⚠️ Data Attribution Gap Identified

Critical Finding: There is a significant discrepancy between reported paid acquisitions (7,884 accounts) and accounts matched in Snowflake (49 accounts). This 99.4% gap suggests:

  • Attribution Delay: New accounts may not yet be synced to the data warehouse
  • Media Source Tagging: Most accounts may be tagged as "Unavailable" rather than proper campaign sources
  • Integration Issues: Potential disconnect between ad platforms and internal attribution systems

Recommendation: Immediate review of attribution pipeline and data sync processes is required to ensure accurate campaign performance tracking.

  2. Scale Apple Search Ads: With best-in-class CPA ($20.69) and CPI ($5.38), increase ASA budget by 30-50% to capitalize on high-intent users.
  3. Optimize Meta Campaigns: Address the high CPA ($31.49) through creative testing, audience refinement, and landing page optimization. Set a 2-week timeline for 20% CPA improvement or consider budget reallocation.
  4. Test TikTok Scale: With strong CPA performance ($21.59), gradually increase TikTok spend by 25% to validate scalability without compromising efficiency.

Medium-Term Initiatives:

  3. Platform Budget Reallocation: Consider shifting Meta's underperforming budget proportionally to ASA (40%), TikTok (30%), and Google (30%) for improved blended CPA.
