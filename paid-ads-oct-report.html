Key Insights & Recommendations

Strategic Observations

  • Consistent CPI Performance: Remarkably uniform CPI across all platforms ($23.78-$24.22) suggests effective bidding strategies and audience targeting alignment.
  • Google Ads Leadership: Capturing 46.9% of total spend and 46.9% of installs, Google Ads demonstrates strong efficiency and scale potential.
  • TikTok Engagement: Despite lower spend share (13.5%), TikTok shows promising CTR (1.61%), indicating high creative engagement with younger demographics.
  • Apple Search Ads Intent: Highest CTR (2.65%) reflects strong user intent from search-driven discovery, valuable for conversion optimization.
  • Activation Performance: An 18.4% activation rate shows solid early user engagement, with opportunities to improve through enhanced onboarding experiences.
  • Product Feature Adoption: Card-based activities (31.4%) and cash load functionality (17.1%) are primary use cases, suggesting these should be highlighted in acquisition messaging.

Recommended Actions

  • Scale Google Ads: Given consistent performance and volume, consider increasing Google Ads budget by 15-20% to capture additional market share.
  • Optimize Meta Creative: Test new creative formats to improve CTR from 1.30% closer to TikTok's 1.61% engagement rate.
  • TikTok Expansion: Strong engagement metrics justify testing 20% budget increase to explore scale potential.
  • Activation Funnel Enhancement: Implement targeted onboarding flows that showcase card and cash load features to improve the 18.4% activation rate.
  • Cross-Platform Testing: Deploy consistent creative messaging across platforms to isolate audience quality differences from creative performance.
  • Product Messaging Alignment: Update ad creative to emphasize card activity and cash loading features, which drive 48.5% of user transactions.
