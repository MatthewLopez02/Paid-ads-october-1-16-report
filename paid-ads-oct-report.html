💡 Key Insights & Recommendations

🎯 Performance Highlights

  • Consistent CPA Across Platforms: All platforms maintained remarkably similar CPAs ($41.39-$44.05), indicating strong cross-platform efficiency in acquisition costs.
  • Liftoff's Superior CVR: Despite having the lowest impression volume, Liftoff achieved an exceptional 35.29% conversion rate, suggesting highly qualified traffic.
  • Google Ads Scale Advantage: With 59% of total impressions and 42% of spend, Google Ads delivered the highest absolute conversion volume (1,876).
  • Apple Search Ads Efficiency: Achieved the highest CTR (0.60%) and second-best CVR (7.12%), indicating strong intent-driven traffic from iOS users.

📈 Strategic Recommendations

  • Scale Liftoff Investment: With the lowest CPA ($41.39) and highest CVR (35.29%), consider gradually increasing Liftoff spend to test scalability while maintaining efficiency.
  • Optimize TikTok Creatives: TikTok's 4.38% CVR shows promise. Test new creative formats and expand successful campaigns to improve the relatively higher CPC of $1.93.
  • Leverage Apple Search Ads: The strong 7.12% CVR and high CTR suggest room for growth. Consider increasing bids on top-performing keywords to capture more high-intent iOS traffic.
  • Meta Audience Refinement: With solid performance but room for improvement, focus on lookalike audience expansion based on high-LTV customer segments.
  • Google Ads Campaign Structure: Maintain current spend levels while optimizing campaign structure. Test Performance Max campaigns to potentially reduce CPC from $0.78.

⚠️ Areas for Monitoring

  • Meta CPC Trends: At $1.30, Meta's CPC is higher than Google Ads. Monitor for seasonal trends and competition impacts as we approach Q4.
  • TikTok Impression Growth: With only 14% of total impressions despite 23% of spend, evaluate if budget reallocation could improve efficiency.
  • Liftoff Scalability: Current low volume (102 conversions) needs testing to determine if quality can be maintained at scale.
  • Cross-Platform Attribution: Consider implementing enhanced tracking to understand cross-device and cross-platform customer journeys.
